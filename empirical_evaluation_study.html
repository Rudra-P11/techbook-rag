The Dense Semantic Drift Phenomenon: Notice that Dense-only retrieval achieved a disappointing 61.1% source accuracy and only 33.3% perfect recall. Why? When queried about specific terminology like "eigenvalues in PCA" (q05) or "symmetric vs asymmetric cryptography" (q15), the embedding vectors drifted toward generic mathematics and security passages in unrelated textbooks, missing the exact chapters. BM25 completely arrested this drift, achieving 94.4% source accuracy.

The Key Optimization Finding:

1. Tuning k = 10 or Weighted RRF (k = 20, w_lex = 1.5): Boosted Term Recall from 88.6% up to 90.8% and Perfect Recall from 61.1% to 66.7%!

2. Mathematical Rationale: Lower k = 10 creates a steeper hyperbolic decay gradient (1/11 ≈ 0.0909 vs 1/61 ≈ 0.0164). This grants higher weight to rank #1–#3 exact keyword rescues from BM25, allowing them to overcome moderate dense rank drift without losing dense conceptual coverage.
